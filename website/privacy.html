Draft — not ready for user acceptance

The operator, jurisdiction, contact details, and signup data practices are still being confirmed. This page must be finalized and legally reviewed before it is used in onboarding.

1. Who is responsible

The operator responsible for Bowser-operated services is [legal operator name and address]. Privacy contact: [privacy email]. This draft describes the current local-browser design and identifies details needed before email onboarding launches.

2. Browser data on your Mac

Bowser stores browser state locally, including tabs, profiles, settings, mods, and website data. Websites can store cookies and other data through the browser engine. Local logs and mod workspaces can contain URLs, errors, prompts, or other activity details. Removing the application alone may leave this data behind.

Visiting a website sends network requests to that website and its services. The website’s own privacy practices apply. Local browser storage does not mean that websites, installed mods, or AI tools cannot transmit data.

3. AI customization and mods

When you use ModSmith, your instructions and tool results can be processed by the AI provider or router you configure. Tool results may include tab information, page text or HTML, screenshots, mod code, and relevant stored values. This can include information from a signed-in website. The provider’s account settings, retention rules, and privacy policy apply to its processing.

Provider sign-in should complete through the provider’s own authentication flow. API keys or router credentials you configure must be treated as secrets. Mods are executable code and may communicate with services of their own; review the sources and services you choose.

4. Email onboarding — proposed, not yet active

If Bowser introduces email signup, the notice shown at collection must state whether email is required, what service it enables, and how to use the browser without that service when available. The proposed signup record includes the email address, verification status, and the Terms version and time accepted. Additional collection must be disclosed before launch.

Account verification and essential service messages are separate from optional marketing. Marketing preferences should be independent of Terms acceptance. Signing into Gmail, Outlook, or another mailbox in a browser tab does not verify a Bowser account unless you complete Bowser’s verification link or code.

5. Website, support, and service providers

Opening the Bowser website or downloading the app involves requests to its hosting provider. The current landing-page stylesheet also loads fonts from Google Fonts. Those requests expose network information such as your IP address to the receiving service. These draft legal pages use system fonts and make no font-service request.

If you contact support or send diagnostic files, the message and any attachments may contain personal information. Share only what is needed to investigate the issue.

Before launch, specify: the hosting, email, analytics or crash-reporting, payment, and support providers actually used; the data each receives; the purpose and lawful basis where applicable; international transfers and safeguards; and any legally required representatives.

6. Retention, deletion, and your choices

Website sessions and local browser files remain subject to the browser’s data controls and your device’s backups. Provider-side AI records are governed by the relevant provider. Deleting local Bowser files does not automatically delete data held by websites or AI services.

Before collecting signup data, specify: retention periods for unverified email addresses, active and deleted accounts, Terms-acceptance records, support requests, and service logs; the account-deletion method; and how deletion applies to backups and required legal records.

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, or obtain a copy of personal data, object to processing, or withdraw consent where processing relies on consent. Contact [privacy email]. The final notice must identify the applicable complaint process and supervisory authority where required.

7. Children and changes

Before launch: confirm the intended age group and any age restrictions or parental-authorization requirements. Do not present an unsupported age threshold as a settled policy.

The final notice will identify its effective date and explain how material changes are communicated. This draft is not an active privacy notice for an email-signup service.
