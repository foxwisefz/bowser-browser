01 — The idea

Most browsers ask you to adapt to them. Bowser adapts to you.

Its interface and behavior can evolve without waiting for a vendor, an extension review, or the next release. Your browser becomes a personal instrument instead of a sealed appliance.

02 — Under the hood

Native where it matters.
Alive where it counts.

01
⌘ K   Search or command…

A truly native shell

AppKit owns the windows, tabs, and keyboard. WKWebView brings modern web compatibility. The result feels at home on the Mac.

02
BEAM mod mod mod

Mods that stay alive

Each mod runs as a supervised Elixir process. Edit one and it hot-reloads. Break one and it restarts alone—not your browser.

03

Your session remembers

The brain preserves tabs and session state while the shell comes and goes. Updates feel more like a blink than a restart.
